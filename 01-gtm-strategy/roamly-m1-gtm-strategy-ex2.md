# Roamly Groups · GTM Strategy, Module 1 (Exercise 2)

## Responses
- **Which motion did you choose, PLG, SLG, CLG, or a hybrid? Explain why; if hybrid, what are your primary vs. secondary layers?:** Hybrid: PLG primary, CLG secondary. Within a trip, buyer and user are the same people, time to value is fast, and it's self-serve — one family member invites the rest of the household group in via the product itself. Across trips, growth also benefits from peer influence: extended families naturally recommend what worked to other family groups at gatherings and reunions, which is advocacy-driven, not product-mechanical. Primary layer: PLG for adoption within a trip. Secondary layer: CLG for spreading to new family groups.
