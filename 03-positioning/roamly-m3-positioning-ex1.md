# Roamly Groups · Positioning Framework & Statement, Module 3 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **FOR:** existing Roamly travelers organizing a multi-household extended family trip
- **WHO WANT / NEED:** to coordinate and book as a group on Roamly — syncing availability, splitting payments, aligning across households — without the product getting in their way and forcing them into separate accounts or manual workarounds
- **WE OFFER:** One shared plan that replaces separate accounts, spreadsheets, and manual payment splitting
- **BECAUSE OF:** less coordination work for the organizer, from day one — even if not every household fully adopts it
- **DESPITE:** not matching the planning depth of legacy tools
- **BETTER THAN:** Planning tools that only help you plan — Roamly turns the plan into a booked experience
