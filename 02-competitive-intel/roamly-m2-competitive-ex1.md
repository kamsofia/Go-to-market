# Roamly Groups · Competitive Battlecard & Strategic Bet, Module 2 (Exercise 1)

## Responses
- **Competitor’s name:** TripProf
- **Their core offering:** All-in-one group trip planning app — day-by-day itinerary builder with map view, multi-currency expense splitting with automated settlement, smart packing checklists, document/passport storage, offline access, and shared trip memories. No booking or marketplace layer — it organizes a trip, it doesn't sell anything inside it.
- **How they position themselves:** "A whole trip, not just the bill" — breadth and cohesion across the entire trip lifecycle. Explicitly markets a family-trip/reunion playbook for single or multi-family travel.
- **Their primary audience:** Groups already committed to traveling together who need to plan and reconcile costs — families, reunions, friend groups. Organizer-led, so there's a clear owner coordinating multiple households.
- **Known pricing or packaging:** Freemium + credit economy, not fully free. Free tier: splitting, itinerary, checklists, limited memories, 13 of 60+ "destination insights." Paid unlocks deeper insights via pay-as-you-go credits (€3.99/credit, down to €2.80 at volume) or Pro at €7.99/mo (3 credits/mo). Collaboration and multi-person planning sits behind the paid tier — not free by default.
- **Why they’re your biggest threat:** Already owns the "whole family trip, multiple households" framing Roamly wants, with itinerary + splitting bundled and a live monetization engine funding continued development — not a stalled hobby app. Their gap: no experience marketplace, and the thing families actually need most (collaboration) is paywalled. If they add experience booking, or a competitor bundles it in, they threaten Roamly's core, not just the coordination layer.
- **Looking at this battlecard, what is the one thing that makes you want to go back and adjust your M1 strategy, and why? “I want to revisit [x] because”:** I want to revisit the scope of the value proposition because the battlecard showed that coordination alone (splitting, itinerary) is something a free tool like TripProf already does well. If Groups only solves coordination, families could use it to organize the logistics and still book activities elsewhere — I can't force the booking step, so the value prop needs to make booking through Roamly the natural next step inside the same flow, not a separate ask after the fact. The segment, motion, and Retain goal all still hold; I just want to make sure the feature itself doesn't stop at "we're coordinated now" without carrying that straight into a booked experience.
